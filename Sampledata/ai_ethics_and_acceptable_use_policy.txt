KNOVERA ENTERPRISE AI ETHICS & ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Document ID: POL-2026-AIE-01
Classification: Confidential - Internal Enterprise
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Author: Knovera Responsible AI & Governance Committee

1. OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
This policy defines the acceptable, responsible, and safe utilization of Knovera Artificial Intelligence (AI) platforms, large language model copilots, and machine learning endpoints. This applies to all employees, contractors, developers, and third-party vendors accessing enterprise data.

2. CORE PRINCIPLES OF RESPONSIBLE AI
Knovera adheres to four foundational tenets of AI governance:
a) Grounded Truthfulness: All generative AI outputs utilized in client deliverables or internal decisions must be corroborated by indexed enterprise sources.
b) Hallucination Guardrails: AI responses lacking direct retrieval citations exceeding the relevance threshold must be refused or labeled ungrounded.
c) Zero Sensitive Data Exfiltration: Personally Identifiable Information (PII), biometric markers, and raw cryptographic credentials must never be passed to external uncached models without tokenization.
d) Algorithmic Accountability: An identifiable team member remains responsible for every production code change or administrative approval assisted by AI tools.

3. PROHIBITED USES
Under no circumstances may Knovera AI systems be used for:
- Automated generation of defamatory, malicious, or deceptive communications.
- Bypassing multi-factor authentication or penetrating internal boundary defenses.
- Training public foundation models on proprietary customer datasets without legal written authorization.
- Real-time biometric surveillance or automated personnel disciplinary actions without human review.

4. MODEL RETRIEVAL & AUDIT LOGGING
All queries submitted through the Knovera conversational portal are logged to the MongoDB Audit Trail. Logs record the query timestamp, latency, token count, guardrail confidence score, and document citation links. Audit logs are preserved for 365 calendar days for compliance audits.

5. VIOLATIONS & REPORTING
Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy will result in immediate API credential revocation and escalated disciplinary review. Potential safety breaches must be reported immediately to compliance@knovera.ai.
