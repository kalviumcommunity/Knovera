KNOVERA CUSTOMER SERVICE & REFUND POLICY
Document Reference: POL-2026-089
Effective Date: January 15, 2026
Version: 3.2 (Enterprise Edition)

1. Overview & Purpose
Knovera is committed to delivering world-class enterprise Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions and customer support. This policy outlines our standard customer service response times, eligible refund windows, billing cancellation procedures, and enterprise escalation pathways.

2. Service Level Agreements (SLAs) & Technical Support
Our global engineering and support staff adhere to strict incident response commitments:
- Tier 1 Technical Inquiries (Account, Billing, General Questions): Initial verified response within 2 business hours.
- Tier 2 Specialized Engineering (Vector Ingestion, API Integration, Guardrail Tuning): Initial response within 6 business hours.
- Emergency Severity 1 Incidents (Production Outage, Complete Service Disruption): 24/7 dedicated response within 15 minutes with continuous hourly status updates.

3. Customer Refund & Cancellation Criteria
- 100% Full Refund Window: All customers and organizations are eligible for a 100% full refund within a 30-day window from the initial subscription activation date, no questions asked.
- Pro-Rated Refund Window: Refund requests submitted after 30 days but before 60 days from activation are subject to a pro-rated service fee calculation based on unused calendar days.
- Beyond 60 Days: No refunds will be issued for billing cycles beyond 60 days. However, accounts can be paused or cancelled at any time without additional penalty or hidden cancellation fees.
- Refund Processing Time: Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method (Credit Card, Corporate ACH, Wire Transfer) within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Subscription Pauses & Downgrades
- Customers may pause active subscriptions for up to 90 calendar days without losing indexed vector collections or custom guardrail configurations.
- Downgrading to the Developer Free tier takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

5. Dispute Resolution & Contact Information
For refund inquiries, billing discrepancies, or escalation requests, please contact:
- Billing Support: billing@knovera.ai
- General Inquiries: support@knovera.ai
- Enterprise Hotline: +1 (800) 555-KNOV (Available 24/7 for Enterprise Plan customers)
