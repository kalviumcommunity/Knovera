KNOVERA ENTERPRISE SLA & SERVICE CREDIT POLICY
Document Reference: SLA-2026-V4
Applicable Services: Knovera RAG Assistant, MongoDB Vector Storage, Ingestion Engine

1. Service Availability Guarantee
Knovera guarantees a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.95% for all Enterprise Plan production endpoints and vector database clusters.

2. Downtime Service Credit Calculation
If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below our guaranteed SLA threshold during any given billing month, customers are entitled to the following financial service credits:
- 99.0% to 99.94% Monthly Uptime: 10% credit applied against the monthly invoice fee.
- 98.0% to 98.99% Monthly Uptime: 25% credit applied against the monthly invoice fee.
- Less than 98.0% Monthly Uptime: 50% credit applied against the monthly invoice fee.

3. Credit Claim Procedures
To receive an SLA credit, customers must submit a claim ticket to billing@knovera.ai within fourteen (14) calendar days following the end of the affected calendar month. The claim must include dates, incident times, and affected API endpoints.

4. Exclusions
Downtime resulting from scheduled maintenance (announced with at least 48 hours notice), customer internet connectivity failures, or force majeure events are excluded from downtime calculations.
