# Knovera Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Domestic Flight Bookings
Employees traveling on approved company business must book economy class domestic flights at least 14 days in advance through the corporate travel portal. Any flight upgrades require prior written authorization from department directors.

## Daily Meal & Incidental Allowance
A daily per-diem meal allowance of $75 is provided for domestic travel ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $30 dinner). Original itemized receipts must be submitted via the expense portal within 15 business days of travel completion.

## Hotel & Lodging Standards
Standard single-occupancy hotel rooms up to $200 per night (excluding taxes) are reimbursable in major metropolitan zones.
