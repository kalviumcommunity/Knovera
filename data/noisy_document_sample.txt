2. Scalable Ingestion Pipeline
Our intake engine processes documents in parallel across dis-
tributed worker nodes. Each worker normalizes line endings, strips page boilerplate, and performs 
unicode NFKC canonical transfor-
mation.

Key Engineering Rules:
  • Preserve code snippets and numeric figures intact.
  • Never remove punctuation aggressively.
  • Maintain section headers to assist semantic chunk boundary detection.
