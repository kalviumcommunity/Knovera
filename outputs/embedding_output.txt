EXPLANATION NOTE:
1. NUMERIC REPRESENTATION OF SEMANTIC MEANING:
   Embedding vectors translate textual concepts into dense, high-dimensional numeric coordinate spaces.
   Unlike arbitrary database auto-increment IDs or sparse keyword counts (e.g., Bag-of-Words / One-Hot encodings),
   embeddings map semantic relationships into geometric proximity.

2. GEOMETRIC NEIGHBORHOODS & KEYWORD INDEPENDENCE:
   Texts that convey identical or related intent ('reset password' vs 'recover login access') land close to
   each other in vector space (high cosine similarity ~0.80 - 0.99), even when sharing zero exact vocabulary words.
   Conversely, texts discussing unrelated topics ('cafeteria menu') lie nearly orthogonal in vector space (~0.00).

3. WHY EMBEDDINGS ENABLE SEMANTIC SEARCH IN RAG:
   Traditional keyword search fails when users ask questions using different vocabulary than the corpus documents.
   In a RAG assistant, chunked documents and user questions are both converted to embedding vectors.
   Retrieval becomes a nearest-neighbor vector search, finding document chunks that match the *meaning* of
   the query regardless of exact phrasing.
